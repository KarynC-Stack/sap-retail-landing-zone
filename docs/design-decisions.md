# Design decisions

This file records where the build intentionally differs from, or adds to, the LZ Solution Offering
Partner Questionnaire. Audit reviewers should read it alongside `audit/matrix.md`.

| # | Date | Questionnaire | Decision | Why |
|---|------|---------------|----------|-----|
| 1 | 2026-11-18 | Q1 OU tree: Security, Workloads → Kenya / Uganda | Adds an **Infrastructure OU** (Network account), a **Sandbox OU**, and **Production / Non-Production OUs** under each country OU. Every OU in Q1 is still present. | Separates shared network services from workloads. Lets Non-Production SCPs attach at OU level rather than per account. Gives experimentation an isolated place. |
| 2 | 2026-11-18 | Q5: each country's workloads in their own VPC | Unchanged: every workload account owns its VPC. In addition, a **Network account** hosts shared services such as Transit Gateway, shared with workload accounts through AWS RAM. | Answers the Q5 prompt about a dedicated network account and inter-VPC/hybrid connectivity. It doesn't change any workload VPC. |
| 3 | 2026-11-18 | Q2: "Uganda OU: restrict to designated Uganda region" | The designated Uganda Region is **eu-west-1 (Ireland)**. | AWS has no Region in Uganda. Ireland matches Kenya and the Control Tower home Region. |
| 4 | 2026-11-18 | Q1/Q2: Uganda accounts | The Uganda OUs exist. **No Uganda accounts are created yet.** | Customer decision. Accounts are vended into Uganda/Production and Uganda/Non-Production when approved. |
| 5 | 2026-11-24 | Not in questionnaire | The **Data Platform** account (<EXCLUDED_ACCOUNT_ID>) stays in its original organization and is **out of scope** for this landing zone. | It is a live production workload. Migrating it needs its own approved change window, so it is neither invited nor enrolled here. |
| 6 | 2026-11-24 | Q1/Q8: Control Tower defined in CloudFormation | A **new** landing zone (v4.0) is created from CloudFormation (`AWS::ControlTower::LandingZone`) in a **new organization** `<ORGANIZATION_ID>` under management account <MANAGEMENT_ACCOUNT_ID>. Supersedes the earlier plan to import an existing landing zone. | The earlier organization was built by hand and its StackSet rollout failed repeatedly. A clean organization gives the whole landing zone a single CloudFormation origin, which is what the questionnaire asks to evidence. |
| 7 | 2026-11-24 | Q1: account emails | Four **new** accounts were created with new primary emails rather than migrating the original accounts. CI locks the new emails (`scripts/repo-policy.ps1`). | Customer decision after account isolation proved impractical. Primary email is create-only in AWS, so CI guards it against accidental edits. |
| 18 | 2026-11-24 | Q2/Q6: write-once replication mechanism | The replication configuration on Control Tower's log bucket is applied by a **CloudFormation custom resource** (inline Lambda in `log-archive.yaml`), not a one-off CLI call. Delete is a deliberate no-op so removing the stack cannot silently stop archiving. | The configuration sits on a bucket Control Tower owns, so CloudFormation cannot declare it directly. A custom resource keeps it in code and re-applies it on every update, which a landing zone reset would otherwise revert unnoticed. Replication covers new objects only. |
| 19 | 2026-11-24 | Q4/Q6/Q7: deployment order | Security services, the log archive and cost/tag governance are deployed **before** the SCPs. | `security-services-protection` denies `guardduty:UpdateOrganizationConfiguration` and `log-archive-protection` denies `s3:PutBucketObjectLockConfiguration`, which are the calls those deployments make. SCPs also block manual cleanup of `sr-lz-*` resources in member accounts, so failures must be fixed before they are attached. |
| 20 | 2026-11-24 | Q4: GuardDuty EBS malware protection | **Not enabled.** | AWS rejects the organization-wide setting from the delegated administrator until the management account enables it for the organization first. Revisit with a cost decision, since scans are billed per GB. |
| 21 | 2026-11-24 | Q7: tag enforcement | The tag policy is **report-only**. No SCP denies untagged launches. | Customer decision. The previous organization had an enforcing SCP; this one deliberately does not. Non-compliant resources are reported, not blocked. |
| 22 | 2026-11-24 | Q1: naming convention | Account display names and SCP names carry **no `sr-` / `SAP_Retail Dev` prefix**: accounts are `Log Archive`, `Security Tooling`, `Networking` and `Kenya Non-Production`, and SCPs are named for what they do (`root-protection`, `region-kenya`, and so on). OU names were already unprefixed. | Customer decision: the prefix added nothing in the console. Accounts were renamed live with `aws account put-account-name`. **CloudFormation cannot do this**: a change set showed `Modify` with no replacement, but the handler then refused with "you must sign in as the root user", so the update rolled back. `AccountName` in `organization.yaml` therefore still holds the original create-time name and is not the live name. Other resources (StackSets, IAM roles, buckets, permission sets, tag policy) keep their `sr-lz-` names because renaming them replaces the resource. |
| 17 | 2026-11-24 | Q3: least privilege | **Overrides decision 11 in part.** At the customer's explicit request, the `SR-Administrators` Identity Center group holds a **standing** `SR-Admin` (`AdministratorAccess`) assignment on every account in the organization, rather than break-glass-only. Session duration stays at 1 hour. | Customer decision: the platform operator needs working cross-account access, and the organization currently has one human operator. Reviewers should treat this as an accepted exception, not an oversight. Revisit when a second operator or a service desk exists, and narrow day-to-day work to `SR-PowerUser`. |
| 14 | 2026-11-24 | Q2/Q6: write-once logs | Control Tower's managed log bucket has versioning but **no Object Lock**, and Object Lock cannot be added to an existing bucket. Write-once is therefore delivered by **S3 replication** from the Control Tower log bucket into a separate Object Lock bucket (`templates/05-logging/log-archive.yaml`). | Keeps decision 13's Control Tower-managed buckets while still giving an immutable archive. AWS documents cross-bucket replication as the supported way to centralize Control Tower logs, and discourages editing the managed bucket's policy. |
| 15 | 2026-11-24 | Q1: OU governance coverage | `AWSControlTowerBaseline` is **not enabled on the Security OU**. Control Tower rejects it: "cannot be enabled on the Security OU". Those accounts are governed by `LogArchiveBaseline`, `CentralSecurityRolesBaseline`, `CentralConfigBaseline` and `IdentityCenterBaseline` instead. | Service limitation, not a gap. The Security OU is the foundational OU. An auditor reading "not enabled" against the Security OU should read this row. |
| 16 | 2026-11-24 | Q2/Q6: AWS Config baseline | `ConfigBaseline` shows "not enabled" on registered OUs. This is expected: AWS describes its resources as a **subset** of `AWSControlTowerBaseline`, which is what OU registration enables. | Enabling both would be redundant. Config recording is active through `AWSControlTowerBaseline`. |
| 23 | 2026-11-24 | Q4: Security Hub standards scope | Two choices. (1) The Security Hub finding aggregator uses **`NO_REGIONS`**: only eu-west-1 is in scope. (2) The `sr-lz-fsbp-cis` configuration policy (AWS Foundational Security Best Practices and CIS v3.0.0) is associated with the **Security, Infrastructure, Workloads and Sandbox OUs**, not the organization root, so the **management account is not covered** by Security Hub standards. | Observed twice on 2026-11-24: a ROOT association with an `ALL_REGIONS` aggregator stayed PENDING for about 50 minutes, then failed with "4 organizational units or accounts under this root failed", and CloudFormation rolled the whole Security Tooling StackSet back. AWS's per-target message for the management account was "Security Hub is not enabled in the organization management account in Regions ap-south-1, eu-west-3 and 15 other(s)": the policy has to be enabled in every linked Region, and `ALL_REGIONS` links 17. After the aggregator was set to `NO_REGIONS` the Security, Log Archive and Security Tooling targets went to `SUCCESS` within minutes. The management account also has no Config recorder (Control Tower does not create one there), so it stays out of scope. Revisit if it should be scanned: enable Security Hub and a Config recorder there first. |
| 24 | 2026-11-24 | Q4: GuardDuty member enrolment | GuardDuty is **enabled in the management account** and every member account is enrolled with `create-members` from Security Tooling. | The organization setting `AutoEnableOrganizationMembers: ALL` did not enrol the existing accounts: `list-members` was empty an hour after it was set. New accounts are expected to auto-enable. The management account first needed its own detector ("your organization master must first enable GuardDuty"). Done by CLI because GuardDuty membership has no CloudFormation resource type. |
| 13 | 2026-11-24 | Q2/Q6: centralized logging | Log and access-log buckets are **created and managed by Control Tower** in the Log Archive account (365-day log retention, 3650-day access-log retention) rather than a bring-your-own bucket. | Customer decision. Removes a cross-account bucket prerequisite before landing zone creation. Decision 8's Object Lock applies to the separately managed write-once archive, not to Control Tower's own buckets. |
| 8 | 2026-11-18 | Q2/Q6: write-once logs | Object Lock **GOVERNANCE** mode, 1460-day default retention. `s3:BypassGovernanceRetention` is denied to everyone by SCP. | Write-once in practice, while staying recoverable by the management account in a legal or regulatory emergency. COMPLIANCE mode can't be undone. |
| 9 | 2026-11-18 | Q4: GuardDuty org-wide | Enabled through `scripts/enable-org-features.ps1` (delegated admin + auto-enable ALL). | There is no CloudFormation resource type for GuardDuty organization settings. |
| 10 | 2026-11-18 | Q5: Kenya Production VPC | The live Kenya Production VPC and VPN stay in the SAP workload repo (`retail-iac/templates/network.yaml`). This repo's `workload-vpc.yaml` is the same pattern for new accounts (Uganda). | Avoids re-deploying live SAP networking. |
| 12 | 2026-11-18 | Q8: validate-policy gate | Two Access Analyzer findings are accepted, scoped to one resource each: `PASS_ROLE_WITH_STAR` on `BreakGlassAdministrator` and on `CloudFormationExecutionRole` (`scripts/validate-policies.ps1`). Every other finding blocks the PR. | Both roles need AdministratorAccess by design. Break-glass has MFA-only trust and alerting. The execution role is assumable only by CloudFormation in the management account. |
| 11 | 2026-11-18 | Q3: permission sets | Control Tower's own Identity Center permission sets (`AWS*`) stay, because Control Tower manages them, but humans are not assigned to them after cutover. Day-to-day access uses only the `SR-*` sets. | Control Tower access management stays enabled. Removing its sets would cause drift. |
